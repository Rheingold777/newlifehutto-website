We'd love to hear from you

We're Real People — Just Reach Out

New Life Hutto is a small church, and that means when you contact us, you're not going through a phone tree or an anonymous inbox. Pastor Bernhard personally reads every message. Whether you have a question about our beliefs, want to know what a service is like, or just need someone to talk to — we're here.

You don't need to be a member, a visitor, or even sure what you believe. If you have something on your mind, reach out. No judgment, no sales pitch.
