"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." 2 Corinthians 9:7

Why We Give

At New Life Hutto, giving isn't about building a budget — it's about building the Kingdom. We're a small church, and that means every dollar matters. Your generosity directly supports our ability to keep the doors open, keep the lights on, and keep reaching families in Hutto and the surrounding communities with the gospel of Jesus Christ.

We believe in being transparent about where your money goes. As a church affiliated with the United Pentecostal Church International, we support global missions, local outreach, and the day-to-day operations of a church that's here to serve our community. There are no hidden agendas — just honest stewardship of what God provides through people like you.

If you're visiting for the first time, please know that you are absolutely not expected to give. This is for our regular members and those who feel led to support what God is doing here. No pressure, no guilt — ever.
