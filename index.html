We're a Small Church
and That's on Purpose

At New Life Hutto, everybody knows your name. We're not trying to be the biggest church in town — we're trying to be a family. A place where you actually belong, not just attend.

We worship hard, pray for each other by name, and believe God still moves the same way He did in the book of Acts. If that sounds like what you've been looking for, come see for yourself.

More About Us

Real People.
Real God.

This is what it looks like when God shows up. Lives changed, families healed, breakthroughs that only heaven can explain.

We're an Apostolic Pentecostal church — we believe in repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, and the gift of the Holy Ghost. Not because it's tradition, but because we've seen it transform lives right here in Hutto.
