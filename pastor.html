Our Pastor

Meet Pastor Bernhard Suppan

Pastor Bernhard Suppan

Pastor Bernhard Suppan leads New Life Hutto with a heart for reaching souls, connecting people to God, and renewing lives through the power of Jesus Christ.

His passion is to see every person experience the transforming power of God's Word and the infilling of the Holy Ghost. Under his leadership, New Life Hutto has become a place where anyone can encounter God, regardless of their background.
