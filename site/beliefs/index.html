What We Believe

Rooted in Apostolic truth

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God — the infallible rule of faith and conduct (2 Timothy 3:16).

One God

We believe in one God who has revealed Himself as Father in creation, Son in redemption, and Holy Ghost in emanation. Jesus Christ is God manifested in the flesh (1 Timothy 3:16, Deuteronomy 6:4, Colossians 2:9).

Salvation

We believe the plan of salvation is revealed in Acts 2:38, and consists of three essential experiences:

  • Repentance — A genuine turning from sin toward God. Repentance is more than remorse; it is a complete change of heart and direction, surrendering your will to the lordship of Jesus Christ.
  • Water Baptism — By immersion in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins. Following the pattern established in the Book of Acts, we baptize in the name of Jesus Christ as the apostles did (Acts 2:38, Acts 8:16, Acts 10:48, Acts 19:5).
  • The Holy Ghost — Received with the initial evidence of speaking in other tongues as the Spirit gives utterance. The gift of the Holy Ghost is for every believer, and it is the power of God living within us to transform, guide, and empower our daily lives (Acts 2:4, Acts 10:46, Acts 19:6).

This is the same gospel preached by the apostles on the Day of Pentecost and throughout the New Testament church. It has never changed, and it still transforms lives today.

"Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost." Acts 2:38

Holy Living

We believe in living a life of holiness and separation from the world, in accordance with biblical standards (Hebrews 12:14, 1 Peter 1:15-16).

Our Affiliation

New Life Hutto is affiliated with the United Pentecostal Church International, the largest Oneness Pentecostal organization in the world. We are committed to the whole gospel for the whole world.
