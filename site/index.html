A CHURCH FAMILY IN HUTTO

Known by name.
Loved as you are.

We're a small church where people pray for one another, worship together, and make room for each other's lives.

We're Apostolic Pentecostal. We believe in repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, and the gift of the Holy Ghost. Our hope is that you encounter Jesus and find people to walk with you.
