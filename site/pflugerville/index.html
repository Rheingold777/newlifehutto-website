A Family Church Worth the Short Drive from Pflugerville

Pflugerville is growing fast, and if you're new to the area — or you've been here a while and haven't found the right church home — we'd love for you to visit New Life Hutto. We're a small, Spirit-filled Apostolic Pentecostal church about 15 miles north of Pflugerville, in Hutto.

What sets us apart? We're genuinely small on purpose. Everybody knows your name here. Our worship is authentic, our prayers are personal, and we believe God still moves the same way He did in the book of Acts. If you've been looking for a Pentecostal church near Pflugerville where you can grow in your faith and be part of a real community, this is it.

Our services are bilingual — we worship in both English and Spanish — and we welcome families from Pflugerville, Round Rock, Austin, and every city in between.

Apostolic Pentecostal Worship Near Pflugerville

New Life Hutto is affiliated with the United Pentecostal Church International (UPCI). We hold to the Apostolic faith — the Oneness of God, salvation through repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, and the infilling of the Holy Ghost with the evidence of speaking in tongues, just as the early church experienced in Acts chapter 2.

We're the closest UPCI church to Pflugerville, and our congregation includes families from across the greater Austin area who made the choice to drive a few extra minutes for a church that feels like home.

Directions from Pflugerville

From Pflugerville, the quickest route is TX-130 North to TX-79 East into Hutto. The drive is about 15 miles and takes 18-20 minutes. You can also take US-79 (Palm Valley Blvd) straight through Round Rock and into Hutto.

We're located at 623 W Front St, Hutto, TX 78634. Free parking is available directly in front of the building.
