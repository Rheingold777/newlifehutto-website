A Spirit-Filled Church Minutes from Round Rock

If you live in Round Rock and you're searching for a church that feels like family, New Life Hutto might be exactly what you've been looking for. We're a small, Spirit-filled Apostolic Pentecostal church located just 10 miles north in Hutto — about a 12-minute drive up Highway 79.

We're not a megachurch with programs for every day of the week. We're a tight-knit community where everybody knows your name, the worship is genuine, and God's presence is real. Several of our families drive from Round Rock every week because they found something here they couldn't find closer to home — a church that actually feels like belonging.

Whether you've been in church your whole life or you're stepping through the doors for the first time, you're welcome here. Come as you are. No dress code, no pressure.

Sundays 11:00 AM Worship Service

What Makes Us Different

New Life Hutto is an Apostolic Pentecostal church affiliated with the United Pentecostal Church International (UPCI). We believe in the full gospel message as taught in the book of Acts — repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, and the gift of the Holy Ghost.

We're the only UPCI church in Hutto, and many of our members come from Round Rock, Pflugerville, and Georgetown because they were looking for an Apostolic church where they could worship in Spirit and truth. Our services are bilingual, welcoming both English and Spanish speakers.

Directions from Round Rock

From Round Rock, take TX-79 East (Palm Valley Blvd) north toward Hutto. The drive is about 10 miles and takes 12-15 minutes with no traffic. We're located at 623 W Front St, Hutto, TX 78634. Look for Suite 1800 and the New Life Hutto sign.

Free parking is available directly in front of the building and along Front Street.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is New Life Hutto from Round Rock?

We're approximately 10 miles north of Round Rock, about a 12-15 minute drive via TX-79 East. We're located at 623 W Front St, Hutto, TX 78634.

What are the service times?

Sunday Worship at 11:00 AM and Tuesday Bible Study at 7:00 PM. Both services are bilingual (English and Spanish).

What denomination is New Life Hutto?

We're an Apostolic Pentecostal church affiliated with the United Pentecostal Church International (UPCI). We believe in the Oneness of God, baptism in Jesus' name, and the gift of the Holy Ghost as described in Acts 2:38.

Is there a Pentecostal church near Round Rock?

Yes! New Life Hutto is the closest UPCI Apostolic Pentecostal church to Round Rock, TX. We welcome families from Round Rock, Pflugerville, Hutto, and the surrounding area every week.
