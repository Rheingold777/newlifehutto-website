A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Come comfortable.
Come with questions.

A few practical details before you arrive. If you need help planning your visit, get in touch.

What about my children?+

Children stay with their parents in worship. Families are welcome, and a family room is available if you need a little space.

What should I wear?+

Wear what you're comfortable in. Jeans, a dress or a suit are all welcome.

Is Spanish translation available?+

Sunday services include Spanish translation. Tuesday Bible study is in English, with help available. Let us know if you have a language question before your visit.

Is the building accessible?+

Our suite is wheelchair accessible, with on-site restrooms. Please contact us if you have a specific access need so we can help you plan.

Do I need to sign up first?+

No. You're welcome to come straight to a service. If you'd like to introduce yourself before you arrive, you can email or call us.
