A Spirit-Filled Church Just Down the Road from Taylor

Taylor is a tight-knit community, and if you're looking for a church that has that same small-town, everybody-knows-your-name feel, New Life Hutto is right next door. We're about 10 miles west on Highway 79 — a straight shot, 12 minutes, no highway drama.

We're an Apostolic Pentecostal church where the worship is real, the prayers are personal, and God's power shows up every service. We're not trying to be the biggest church in Williamson County — we're trying to be a family. Several of our members drive from Taylor because they found genuine community here.

Whether you've grown up in church or you're checking things out for the first time, you'll feel at home. No dress code, no judgment — just come as you are.

Apostolic Faith for Taylor Families

New Life Hutto is affiliated with the United Pentecostal Church International (UPCI). We preach the full gospel — repentance, baptism in Jesus' name, and the gift of the Holy Ghost as described in Acts 2:38. It's the same message the early church preached, and we've seen it change lives right here in our community.

Our services are bilingual, welcoming both English and Spanish-speaking families. We're the closest UPCI Apostolic church to Taylor, and we'd love to welcome you.
