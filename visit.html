Your First Sunday

Here's exactly what happens from the moment you pull in. No surprises.

1

Pull Up & Park

Free parking right out front. Look for the New Life Hutto sign at Suite 1800.

2

Get Greeted

Someone will meet you at the door, point you to coffee, and answer any questions.

3

Worship Together

Service runs about 90 minutes — Spirit-filled worship, then a Bible-based message you can actually use.

4

Stick Around

We love to hang out after. Grab a snack, meet the pastor, ask anything. No pressure, no signup.

Good to Know

The practical stuff so you can walk in relaxed.

Parking

Free, on-site, plenty of spaces. No parking lot stress on your first visit.

The Building

We're in a renovated suite — clean, climate-controlled, wheelchair accessible. Restrooms on-site.

Coffee & Snacks

Fresh coffee, water, and a snack table before and after service. Help yourself.

Bilingual

Sunday services include Spanish translation. Tuesday Bible Study runs in English with help available.

Kids Welcome

We love wiggle and noise. Kids stay with you in service — no separate program required. Family room available if needed.

What to Wear

Anything you're comfortable in. Jeans, dress, suit, t-shirt — all welcome. Bernhard usually wears a button-down.

What to Expect

Come As You Are

No dress code. Come comfortable, come as yourself. We care about you, not what you're wearing.

Heartfelt Worship

Expect authentic, Spirit-filled worship. We believe in the power of praise to draw us closer to God.

Relevant Teaching

Bible-based messages applicable to everyday life. You'll leave encouraged and challenged.

Warm Welcome

We're a small, welcoming community. You won't get lost in a crowd. You'll be greeted warmly and feel at home.

Bring the Whole Family

Our kids worship right alongside their parents — we believe families belong together. Grandma's coming? We'll save her the comfy chair.
